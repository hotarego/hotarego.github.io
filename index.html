Why “Hotarego”?

Hotarego (هوتارگو) is the local name of a place in the north-east of Hormozgan province, Iran. Its Persian name is Ābtārikān (آبتاریکان). We named our studio after it.

We make small, handcrafted games that are easy to pick up, satisfying to master and made to be enjoyed at your own pace.

  • Fair, transparent game economies
  • Handcrafted, tested content
  • Cozy art and calm sound
