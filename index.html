Where the name comes from

Hotarego (هوتارگو) is what locals call a place in the north-east of Hormozgan, Iran. In Persian it's Ābtārikān (آبتاریکان). We named ourselves after it.

We like puzzle games you can play on a short break, so that's the kind we make.
